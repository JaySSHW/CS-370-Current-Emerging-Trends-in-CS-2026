## Project Two: Pirate Intelligent Agent

### Project Overview

In this project, I developed an intelligent agent for a treasure hunt game using deep Q-learning, reinforcement learning, and neural networks. The objective was to train a pirate non-player character (NPC) to navigate a maze, avoid obstacles, and locate treasure efficiently. Through this project, I gained practical experience applying artificial intelligence concepts to pathfinding problems and understanding how intelligent systems learn through interactions with their environments.

### What Code Was Provided, and What Did I Create Myself?

The project included starter code that established the treasure hunt environment, defined the maze structure, and provided supporting functionality for the reinforcement learning process. This included components for managing the game environment, tracking the pirate's actions, and evaluating rewards.

My responsibility was to complete the deep Q-learning implementation that allowed the pirate agent to learn an effective path toward the treasure. I worked with the neural network model, implemented the training process, and adjusted parameters to improve the agent's performance. The training process used an exploration and exploitation strategy, allowing the pirate to discover possible paths while gradually learning which actions produced the greatest rewards. Through testing and refinement, the agent demonstrated improved decision-making and achieved a 100% recent win rate during training. This project strengthened my understanding of how reinforcement learning algorithms can solve problems through experience rather than relying entirely on predefined instructions.

### What Do Computer Scientists Do, and Why Does It Matter?

Computer scientists research, design, develop, and improve computational systems that solve problems and support technological innovation. Their work extends beyond writing code to include analyzing complex challenges, designing algorithms, evaluating system performance, and developing solutions that improve efficiency, accessibility, and reliability.

Throughout this course, I learned how artificial intelligence expands the capabilities of traditional computing by allowing systems to recognize patterns, learn from data, and make decisions. Technologies such as neural networks and reinforcement learning have applications in healthcare, robotics, transportation, cybersecurity, and many other industries. Computer scientists play an important role in ensuring these technologies are developed responsibly and provide meaningful benefits to individuals, businesses, and society.

### How Do I Approach a Problem as a Computer Scientist?

My approach begins with understanding the problem, identifying the requirements, and breaking the challenge into smaller, manageable components. I then evaluate possible solutions, select appropriate algorithms and tools, and develop an implementation that can be tested and improved.

The Pirate Intelligent Agent project reinforced the importance of experimentation, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement. Rather than expecting an intelligent agent to perform perfectly from the beginning, I learned to evaluate its behavior, analyze training results, and adjust parameters to improve performance. I also learned that achieving strong results during training does not automatically guarantee success in every possible situation. This experience emphasized the importance of thorough validation and using measurable evidence to determine whether a solution meets its intended objectives.

As I continue pursuing my software engineering degree, I plan to apply this structured approach to future projects by focusing on problem analysis, thoughtful design, testing, and continuous learning.

### What Are My Ethical Responsibilities to the End User and the Organization?

As a future software engineer, I recognize that my responsibilities extend beyond developing functional applications. I have an ethical obligation to protect user privacy, maintain data security, promote fairness, and ensure that the systems I develop are reliable and transparent.

Artificial intelligence introduces additional ethical considerations because intelligent systems may produce unexpected results, reflect biases in their training data, or make decisions that affect users. Developers must carefully evaluate these risks, test systems under different conditions, and communicate limitations rather than presenting automated decisions as infallible.

I also have a responsibility to the organization by following secure coding practices, maintaining accurate documentation, protecting sensitive information, and developing software that meets established requirements. Ultimately, I believe responsible computer science involves balancing technological innovation with accountability, ethical decision-making, and respect for the people who depend on the technology.
